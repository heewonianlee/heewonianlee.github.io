At Michigan State University's HATLab, I am developing a CNN accelerator for speaker verification on the Kria KV260. The design has to fit the FPGA while keeping inference latency low.

## Convolution and memory

I use Vitis HLS to compare ways of organizing the convolution pipeline and banking its memory. For each version, I track latency alongside DSP and block RAM use. This lets me see when a faster design spends more on-chip memory.

## System integration and verification

The Vivado testbench configures the accelerator through its AXI-Lite registers and compares the two output logits with CPU reference values.
